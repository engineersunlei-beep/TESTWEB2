PRODUCTS

Choose the material that fits your application by magnetic field strength, operating temperature, and cost, with in-house FEA analysis to confirm suitability before tooling.

NdFeB Magnets

Made from neodymium, iron, and boron. Delivers the highest maximum energy product (BHmax), more than 10 times that of ferrite, making it the strongest commercially available magnet.

Samarium-Cobalt Magnets

Excellent heat and corrosion resistance, usable at temperatures up to 350°C. Widely used in motors, radar systems, and sensors.

Ferrite Magnets

Made from iron and strontium or barium. Cost-effective thanks to a simple manufacturing process and high iron-oxide content. Commonly used in toys, packaging, and speakers.

Rubber Magnets

Formed from strontium oxide, iron oxide, and chlorinated polyethylene. Ready to use, or can be coated or printed for a better finish.
